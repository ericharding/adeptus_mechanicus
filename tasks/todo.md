## Review — Chapter One motive rewrite (2026-07-31)

Replaced the TBD murder motive. Veth-Karn stole Relic Item 7-Sanctum-Omicron — a disassembled suit of Cataphractii-pattern Tactical Dreadnought armour — from the vault and sold it to the Ashen Hand, an obscure Chapter that could not obtain one legitimately at any price. He falsified the destruction record to close the gap, and killed Enginseer Torvan when Torvan found the crates staged on Sub-Level 4.

Two existing clues constrained the design and both survive verbatim. The Elevator's **seventeen trips in one month** selects a bulky item moved in pieces and makes a Marine Chapter the natural buyer. The **sixty years with no visitors to the vault level** is preserved by routing the armour out on the cargo hoist rather than the elevator; the seventeen elevator trips are the staging on Sub-Level 4, not the extraction.

Chain C's payoff inverted: the reliquary casket is still in the vault, and the armour inside is a stripped husk. The proof is that the relic no longer answers the litany. PORTA-SANCTUM-1 has stood in that silence for thirty years and has it logged, along with a Magos' Rite of Verification by hoist — and its doctrine forbids it from concluding anything from either.

Added the exposure mechanic. No machine informs deliberately; each leaks because leaking is its correct function (APOTHEX-7 files, PORTA-SANCTUM-1 boasts, VOX-ERRANT routes, LAVANT-7 gossips), so the player's own successful puzzle solutions generate the heat and advancement pulls against safety. Chain F now ends by convincing COGITAS-PRIMUS to file, behind a three-part corroboration gate — physical, documentary, and the Elevator's witness — with the Elevator mandatory because it is the only record Veth-Karn never touched.

**Softlock guards:** the Elevator's memory can never be erased, corrupted, or lost in any game state; and mishandling COGITAS-PRIMUS costs the warm route to the endgame, never the endgame — recovery is the complete gate plus an honest acknowledgment, after which it files coldly.
